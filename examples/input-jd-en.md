# Senior Growth Manager

**Northwind Supply** · Series B sustainable home goods · Remote (US), hybrid option in Austin · $140–170K base + 0.15% equity refresh + variable

**About us**: Northwind Supply designs and sells sustainable home goods (kitchen, bath, bedding) direct-to-consumer. We grew from $2M to $52M revenue in four years, and are now scaling post Series B with a dedicated growth function. The team is 40 people, distributed across the US.

**Reports to**: Head of Marketing

## Responsibilities

- Own the end-to-end growth funnel for our DTC store — acquisition, onboarding, retention
- Lead a team of 5–8 growth marketers (paid, lifecycle, referral)
- Manage a $2.4M annual paid budget, holding blended CAC flat at ~$38
- Partner with Product on site optimization and activation flows; with Brand on content and influencer
- Monthly board reporting on funnel health and unit economics

## Requirements

- 7+ years in user growth — DTC e-commerce or consumer SaaS preferred
- Proven track record scaling user acquisition *while holding CAC stable* (not just buying traffic)
- Built and led growth teams of 3+ people with measurable retention
- Data-driven: SQL proficiency, hands-on with at least one BI tool (Looker, Mode, dbt)
- Comfortable owning P&L — you have worked with CAC/LTV payback models before

**Preferred (not required)**

- Shopify Plus experience
- SMS marketing (Klaviyo, Attentive)
- Creator / influencer partnerships at scale

## Equity note

Standard 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff. Equity refresh is **0.15% target** — discretionary based on performance. We will walk through the full cap table and your target dilution during final rounds.

## Work arrangement

Remote-first. We have a small Austin office if you want to come in 1–2 days a week. We will cover home-office setup ($500 stipend) and quarterly team offsites.
